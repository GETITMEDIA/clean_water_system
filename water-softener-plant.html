Water Softener Plants

Our water softener plants are available in manual, semi-automatic and fully automatic operation, chosen based on how much hardness needs to be removed before regeneration is required. In residential systems, hard water increases the likelihood of limescale deposits — pipes get clogged as a result, and the efficiency of hot water boilers and tanks drops, raising domestic water heating costs by roughly 15–20%.

Limescale build-up also damages household appliances such as washing machines over time. Softening your water protects and extends the life of these domestic appliances as well as your plumbing.
